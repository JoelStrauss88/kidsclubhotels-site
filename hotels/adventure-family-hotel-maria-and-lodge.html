Getting there

Recommended transportMilan Malpensa (MXP) is the main international gateway — 2.5–3 hrs by car into the Dolomites valleys. Venice (VCE) suits the eastern Dolomites (2–2.5 hrs); Innsbruck (INN) is closest for the northern valleys. Bolzano has an airport but almost no international routes — avoid routing through it.
