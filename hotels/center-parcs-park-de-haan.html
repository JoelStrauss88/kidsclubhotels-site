Recommended transportBrussels Airport is ~1.5 hrs by car. Ostend Airport is ~10 min by car. Tram along the Belgian coast runs directly to De Haan — one of the few Center Parcs parks accessible without a car.

Seasonal closures

Center Parcs parks are open year-round (52 weeks). There are no seasonal closures — this is a key advantage over Alpine ski hotels.
